Launching an ERC-20 on Elysium and Robinhood Chain

By Graham McCann · 23 November 2026

Both networks run normal ERC-20 contracts, so the token itself is the same as on Ethereum. What changes is the gas coin and how you fund your wallet. Elysium uses HYPE bridged from HyperEVM. Robinhood Chain uses ETH bridged from Ethereum. Deploying a standard token cost about 3 US cents on either network when I measured it, against about $8 on Ethereum mainnet.

What a token deploy really costs

I asked each network's public RPC to estimate the gas for deploying the same fixed-supply ERC-20 contract (name, symbol, decimals, supply, owner), then priced it at that moment's gas price and coin price. The contract needs 763,583 gas on every network. The price per unit of gas is what differs.

Funding your wallet without getting stuck

Most failed launches on new networks fail before the token exists, because the wallet holds the right coin on the wrong network.

  • Elysium: you need HYPE on Elysium itself. HYPE on Hyperliquid or HyperEVM is a separate balance. Get HYPE onto HyperEVM first (an exchange withdrawal to the HyperEVM network, or a cross-chain swap), then bridge it at elysium.kinetiq.xyz/bridge. Use the official bridge only.
  • Robinhood Chain: you need ETH on Robinhood Chain. ETH on Ethereum or Base does not count. Bridge a small amount across before you start.
  • Both: leave a little headroom. The deploy itself is cheap, but the first liquidity pool and a few test transfers all need gas.
  • Only bridge what you plan to use. Withdrawals back out take days on both networks.

Doing it without code

If you would rather not compile and deploy a contract yourself, a no-code creator does the same job from your browser wallet. ETHTokenLaunch has a token creator for Elysium and a token creator for Robinhood Chain. Both deploy the fixed-supply contract measured above, send the whole supply to your wallet and can renounce ownership for you. Either way, check the finished contract on the network's explorer before you share the address.
